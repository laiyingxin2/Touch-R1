01 / The idea

Seeing is not
the whole story.

Touch-R1 brings R1-style reinforcement learning to tactile reasoning. We build TouchReason-1M, a multi-sensor dataset with synchronized tactile-force records and verified reasoning QA, and TouchReason-Bench, a benchmark for tactile perception, ordinal comparison, cross-sensor consistency, and visual-tactile conflict. Touch-R1 combines ordinal-aware accuracy, cross-sensor physical consistency, structured-format control, and input-side tactile grounding, enabling MLLMs to revise visual priors using contact evidence.

Recording protocol & case metadata ↗

These 10 examples were selected for Touch-R1 completion and Original Qwen non-completion: one LIBERO Spatial case and nine MetaWorld cases. They are curated demonstrations, not an aggregate benchmark success-rate sample.

GPT-6 Astra follows the model name supplied with these recordings; the backend model identity has not been independently verified. It uses zero-shot direct control; Qwen and Touch-R1 use action training, with different training budgets.

Source timing is preserved in the files: LIBERO 1× and MetaWorld 0.5×. The default 1× preview plays that source timing; the optional 2× preview multiplies all three methods equally without editing the recordings. Model-inference waiting time is omitted. Outcomes and action-step counts follow the supplied case manifest; clip duration is not an inference-speed measurement.

03 / The method

Three-stage tactile-grounded training.

Learn contact dynamics, connect touch to language, then reinforce reasoning grounded in physical evidence.

Tactile Dynamics

Pretrain a touch encoder by predicting future tactile tokens from contact histories.

QA SFT

Align touch, vision, and language to a structured reasoning interface.

Tactile-adapted GRPO

Reward answers that are correct, consistent, parsable, and tactile-dependent.
